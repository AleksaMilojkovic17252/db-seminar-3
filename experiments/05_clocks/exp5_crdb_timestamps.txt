CockroachDB — Hybrid Logical Clock (HLC)

Gotcha: the runbook's `crdb_internal.cluster_logical_timestamp()` does not
exist in v26.2.0 (SQLSTATE 42883, unknown function). The correct call is
the plain top-level builtin `cluster_logical_timestamp()`, no
`crdb_internal.` prefix.

$ cockroachdb-26 sql --insecure --host=localhost:26257 -e "SELECT cluster_logical_timestamp();"
1785253941830227394.0000000000

$ cockroachdb-26 sql --insecure --host=localhost:26257 -e "SELECT cluster_logical_timestamp();"
1785253944273571133.0000000000

$ cockroachdb-26 sql --insecure --host=localhost:26257 -e "SELECT cluster_logical_timestamp();"
1785253945958757268.0000000000

Monotonically increasing across all three calls, confirming the HLC
advances with each request even though these three calls came from three
separate client connections (no shared transaction state). Screenshot:
results/screenshots/exp5_crdb_clockoffset.png (DB Console -> Metrics ->
Dashboard: Runtime -> Clock Offset). All 3 nodes' offsets fluctuate in the
0-50us range over the observed hour, far under the default --max-offset
of 500ms (500,000us) — expected and not very informative on its own,
since all three nodes are local processes on one laptop sharing the same
system clock; this does not exercise real network clock skew the way a
multi-machine deployment would.
